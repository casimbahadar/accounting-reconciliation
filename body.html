What it checks

A payment is matched when its bank description names an invoice number (INV-1042, INV1042, #1042 or "invoice 1042"), whatever the date. A payment with no number is matched when exactly one open invoice has that balance and is dated within a set number of days before it. When two or more fit, it goes to a person instead of being guessed. It flags payments with no invoice, invoices with no payment, partial, duplicate and extra payments, tax that isn't 13% HST on H13 invoices, tax charged on EXEMPT or ZERO invoices, and totals that don't equal subtotal plus tax. You can match any leftover payment by hand, and undo it.

Bank files can have one signed amount column or separate deposit and withdrawal columns, with or without a header row. Title lines above the header and totals rows are left out.
